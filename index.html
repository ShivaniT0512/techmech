FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products, services, and processes.

What types of conveyor systems do you manufacture?
We manufacture a comprehensive range including flat belt conveyors, inclined belt conveyors, truck loading conveyors, roller conveyors (gravity and powered), screw conveyors, slat chain conveyors, wire mesh conveyors, bucket elevators, pneumatic conveying systems, drum filling machines, tank fabrication, and MS trolleys, pallets & grating. All systems are custom-engineered to meet specific application requirements.
Do you provide custom engineering solutions?
Yes, custom engineering is our core strength. Every project begins with understanding your specific material characteristics, throughput requirements, facility layout, and operational goals. Our engineering team designs solutions tailored to your exact needs.
What is the typical project timeline?
Project timelines vary based on complexity. Standard conveyor systems typically take 4-6 weeks from order confirmation. Complex integrated systems may take 8-12 weeks. We provide detailed timelines during the proposal stage and are committed to on-time delivery.
Do you provide installation and commissioning support?
Yes, we provide complete end-to-end support including site survey, installation, commissioning, operator training, and post-installation maintenance support. Our team works across India to ensure smooth project execution.
Which industries do you serve?
We serve aluminium and copper manufacturing, chemical plants, polymer industries, medical device manufacturing, electrical material manufacturing, technical textile, natural resource companies, and many more. Our solutions are adapted to meet industry-specific requirements and standards.
What after-sales support do you provide?
We provide comprehensive after-sales support including maintenance contracts, spare parts supply, emergency repair services, and technical consultation. Our goal is to ensure maximum uptime and longevity of all installed systems.
